## What changed
Modified `submit_order_with_pg` in `src/services/auto_queue/order_routes.rs` to take `body: OrderBody` (owned) rather than `body: &OrderBody`. This allowed us to consume `body.rationale` and `body.reasoning` directly rather than running `.clone()` on them.

## Why
Submitting orders happens via the `POST /api/queue/runs/{id}/order` hot path. Eliminating the duplicate `String` allocation for `rationale`/`reasoning` slightly reduces latency and heap pressure per submitted order list. This meets the `Bolt` mandate of performing exactly one measurable low-risk performance improvement in an owned hot path.
