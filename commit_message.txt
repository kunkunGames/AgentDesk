ProviderPilot: add observable poison recovery to CancelToken locks

What changed:
Updated all `CancelToken` and adapter lock accesses (in `provider.rs`, `claude.rs`, and `codex.rs`) that previously swallowed lock-poison panics to now use explicit poison recovery, emitting a `tracing::warn!` message before taking the inner guard.

Why:
Silent lock poison recovery masks potential provider-state corruption or thread crashes that shouldn't happen under normal operating circumstances. Making lock recovery observable allows for earlier issue detection in production without crashing live sessions, aligning with the pattern identified in PR #195 and #207.
