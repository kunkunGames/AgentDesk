**What changed**
Modified `dashboard/src/components/OfficeManagerModal.tsx` to use `useFocusTrap(true)` and `useReturnFocus(true)` from `dashboard/src/components/common/overlay/useFocusTrap` and `dashboard/src/components/common/overlay/useReturnFocus`. Attached the `ref` returned by `useFocusTrap` to the modal overlay's root `div` element, and wired up `useEffect` to close the modal on "Escape" key press.

**Why**
The `OfficeManagerModal` component was missing focus trap handling for its modal presentation. Implementing this ensures focus stays within the modal while open, keyboard navigation functions as expected, and focus is restored to the initiating element upon closure, significantly improving the dashboard's accessibility and adherence to ARIA standards.
