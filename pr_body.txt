What changed:
Replaced `.clone()` with `.as_ref()` when unwrapping `state.pg_pool` in the auto-queue view_admin_routes, order_routes, and slot_routes.

Why:
`state.pg_pool` is an `Option<sqlx::PgPool>`. `PgPool` is an `Arc` under the hood. While cloning it is cheap, it unnecessarily increments an atomic reference count on an extremely hot path. `state.pg_pool_ref()` does not exist natively on the `AppState`, so `.as_ref()` is the correct and idiomatically sound way to avoid allocations in standard Axum handlers on hot queue endpoints. This provides a minor but explicit determinisitc reduction in CPU cycles per route hit.
