**What changed:**
Added the missing `aria-modal="true"` attribute to the Cancel Confirmation Modal (`SurfaceCard` component acting as a dialog) within `KanbanStatusModals.tsx`.

**Why:**
While the component correctly sets `role="dialog"`, it was missing `aria-modal="true"`. This attribute correctly instructs assistive technologies that background content is obscured and cannot be interacted with when the modal is open. This improves dashboard accessibility for assistive technology users.
